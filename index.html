Google access, explained.

The app requests permission to read Drive files, read and update spreadsheets, and access files selected or created through the app. It uses these permissions to open attendance PDFs, read rosters and save attendance.

Handwriting recognition runs on the Windows computer. PDFs, roster copies and recovery data are stored locally. Read the privacy policy before connecting your account.

Getting the app

Contact your course coordinator for the Windows installer and access to the shared attendance folder. This website does not process or collect attendance files.
