Using Attendance OCR

Terms of use

Effective October 12, 2026

Use only records you are allowed to handle

Use the app only with permission from the course instructors and the owners of the Google files. Follow your institution’s rules for student records, approved devices and data retention.

Check the results

Handwriting recognition can make mistakes. Review each student match, instructor, date and bin. Check the final Google Sheet before relying on attendance results. A conflict marked ERROR needs human review.

Protect your account and work

Keep your Windows and Google accounts secure. Do not share authorization tokens. Wait for save confirmation before closing the app, and retain original attendance papers according to course instructions.

Availability

The app depends on your computer, internet connection and Google services. It may contain errors or experience interruptions. It does not guarantee perfect recognition or uninterrupted saving.

Privacy and support

Read the privacy policy for details about local files and Google access. For help, contact saifmkhalotaibi@gmail.com.
