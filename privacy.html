Data and account access

Privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Attendance OCR is a Windows application developed by Saif Alotaibi. It helps authorized course staff review attendance papers and record attendance in Google Sheets.

Information the app accesses

When you connect Google, the app receives authorization tokens. It accesses Drive file information, the attendance PDFs you choose, roster names, and the attendance dates and bins in the selected Google Sheet.

The current app requests Drive read-only access, spreadsheet read/write access, and access to files selected or created through the app. These permissions are broader than access to a single attendance folder. Google account and file-sharing permissions still control what your account can access.

How information is used

The app reads selected PDFs, recognizes handwriting, compares names with the roster, and saves confirmed attendance to your selected sheet. It keeps recovery data so you can resume unfinished work. It does not use attendance records for advertising or to train a general-purpose AI model.

Where information is stored

Handwriting recognition runs locally. Selected PDFs, roster copies, settings and saved sessions are stored on your computer, normally in the AttendanceOCR folder under Windows Local AppData. OAuth tokens are encrypted using Windows account protection. This does not mean all cached attendance files are encrypted by the app.

Google Drive and Sheets handle the files and updates sent to their services under your account. The app does not send attendance files to a developer-operated processing server.

Sharing

Results are written to the sheet you choose and are accessible to people who have permission to that sheet. The app does not sell attendance data. If you contact support, the developer receives whatever information you include; avoid sending student records or credentials.

Retention and deletion

Local working files and recovery data remain until deleted. Signing out does not erase attendance caches or sessions. To remove them, finish saving needed results, close the app, and ask your course coordinator or IT staff to remove the appropriate AttendanceOCR local data in accordance with course retention rules. Deleting local recovery files can permanently remove unsaved work.

You can revoke the app’s access from your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revocation does not delete files already in Drive or saved on the computer. Google files must be managed separately by their owner or an authorized editor.

Google user data

Attendance OCR’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This website

This informational website has no attendance upload form, advertising or app-added analytics. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as connection and request logs, to serve and secure the site.

Questions or changes

Contact saifmkhalotaibi@gmail.com with privacy questions or deletion requests relating to information you sent directly to support. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date.
